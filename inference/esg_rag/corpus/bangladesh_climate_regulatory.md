## 1. Environment Conservation Rules, 2023 (ECR 2023)

Promulgated via Bangladesh Gazette Extraordinary, 5 March 2023 (S.R.O. No. 53-Ain/2023) under the Bangladesh Environment Conservation Act, 1995:
- Replaced the legacy 1997 ECR classification with four revamped categories: **Green (সবুজ)**, **Yellow (হলুদ)**, **Orange (কমলা)**, and **Red (লাল)**.
- **Textile wet processing, dyeing, industrial boilers (>5 tons/hr), and captive power plants (>5 MW)** fall under the **Red Category**, requiring mandatory Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), and continuous emission monitoring.
- Mandates annual environmental clearance renewal, greenhouse gas audit trail recordkeeping, and adherence to air emission standards for industrial exhaust stacks.

## 2. Updated Nationally Determined Contributions (NDC, August 2021)

Submitted to the UNFCCC Secretariat under Articles 4.9 and 4.11 of the Paris Agreement:
- **Base Year**: 2012. **Target Year**: 2030.
- **Sectors Covered**: Energy (power, transport, industry, commercial, residential), Industrial Processes and Product Use (IPPU), Agriculture, Forestry and Other Land Use (AFOLU), and Waste.
- **Unconditional Mitigation Target**: Reduce GHG emissions by **27.56 Mt CO2e (6.73%)** below Business-As-Usual (BAU) levels by 2030 using domestic resources.
- **Conditional Mitigation Target**: Reduce GHG emissions by an additional **61.90 Mt CO2e (15.12%)** below BAU by 2030, conditional on international finance, technology transfer, and capacity building. Total combined reduction: **89.46 Mt CO2e (21.85%)** below BAU.
- Specific industrial actions: 10% energy efficiency improvements in designated industrial boilers and motors across the textile, brick kiln, and manufacturing sectors.

## 3. Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission (BERC) & Utility Structures

- Electricity distribution utilities in Bangladesh:
  - **DESCO** (Dhaka Electric Supply Company Limited): Mirpur, Uttara, Gulshan, Banani, Tongi.
  - **DPDC** (Dhaka Power Distribution Company Limited): Central, South Dhaka, Narayanganj.
  - **BPDB** (Bangladesh Power Development Board): Urban areas outside DESCO/DPDC/WZPDCL/BREB, and national generation/single buyer.
  - **BREB** (Bangladesh Rural Electrification Board): Rural areas through Palli Bidyut Samity (PBS).
  - **WZPDCL** (West Zone Power Distribution Company): South-western Bangladesh (Khulna, Barishal).
  - **NESCO** (Northern Electricity Supply Company): Rajshahi, Rangpur divisions.
- Billing components: Active energy (kWh), Maximum Demand Charge (kW), Power Factor Surcharge/Penalty (below 0.95), VAT (5%), and Meter Rent.
- Gas distribution utilities:
  - **Titas Gas Transmission and Distribution Company Limited**: Greater Dhaka and Mymensingh.
  - **KGDCL** (Karnaphuli Gas): Chattogram region.
  - **JGTDSL** (Jalalabad Gas): Sylhet region.
  - **PGCL**, **BGDCL**, **SGCL**: Western and southern regions.
