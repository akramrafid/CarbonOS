# CarbonOS competitive review and website upgrade

Reviewed: 4 November 2026. Branch: `Asif`. Source baseline: `655a067`. Project/team name **CarbonOS** was confirmed by the user; a public contact address and deployment URL were not confirmed.

## Verdict

**There is no evidence that CarbonOS is currently the best product or website in this sector.** The tested prototype has a useful evidence → review → calculation → lock/export path, but substantially narrower coverage and less operational/customer evidence than the offerings advertised by the reviewed vendors. An attractive homepage cannot establish accounting correctness, model accuracy, assurance, or production reliability.

The practical opportunity is a focused, measurable workflow for **Bangladesh factory ESG/compliance teams**. English/Bengali evidence retrieval and a locally relevant demonstration can support that positioning. Neither uniqueness nor customer preference for this approach has been established. The next competitive advantage must come from real document performance, reviewer usability, and accountable delivery.

This increment improves the public product story and the path into the working demo. It does not claim to have caught up with every enterprise capability below.

## Method and limits

- Reviewed six vendors' current official public pages. Feature statements below are vendor-advertised, not independently tested or certified by this review. Package, region, contract, and availability conditions can apply.
- Inspected CarbonOS source and its local rendered homepage, company pages, navigation, workspace entry, source-backed assistant, and pilot brief. Prior tested accounting/RAG evidence is in [AI_SYSTEM_UPGRADE.md](AI_SYSTEM_UPGRADE.md).
- Compared buyer focus, scope, document intake, AI/data controls, reporting, supplier workflows, trust evidence, demo access, pricing clarity, and conversion. This is not an exhaustive market survey, paid-product usability trial, third-party ranking, or competitor speed benchmark.
- No vendor accounts were created, no demos requested, no forms submitted, and no commercial terms accepted. No competing certification, customer count, price, or ROI claim was copied into CarbonOS.

## Implemented from this review

1. **Focused the homepage.** The main page now leads with factory ESG evidence, a real workspace entry, an interactive explanatory workflow, the source-backed assistant, product-scope boundaries, a pilot brief, and practical questions.
2. **Removed unsupported lead-page promises.** The homepage no longer mounts the old client/logo showcase, simulated telemetry station, national-registry/credit guarantees, automated compliance pipeline, abatement portfolio figures, or invented price/SLA tiers. Their source files/routes are retained as legacy work; additional prototype entry points remain available and labeled.
3. **Made the workflow usable.** Four preview steps explain source → review → calculation → lock/export. Values/factors are labeled synthetic; the preview directs users to the real API demo rather than pretending to execute it. The actual sample bill is downloadable.
4. **Added a working pilot planner.** It validates choices and downloads a local discussion brief. Unsupported scans/Scope 3 goals remain explicit gaps. No contact details are collected, no enquiry is submitted, and no price/date/support promise is invented.
5. **Added trust/data context.** `/company/trust`, refreshed data-use/service-status pages, About, and the workflow guide explain implemented controls and unresolved deployment decisions. Removed unsupported Bangladesh-only hosting, legal-enforcement, trading and registry assertions from those lead pages. These prototype notices are not a completed commercial legal/privacy program.
6. **Applied the confirmed CarbonOS name.** Primary navigation, hero watermark, footer, About and metadata use CarbonOS. Some legacy module content still uses earlier names; this is not a full legacy rebrand.
7. **Fixed navigation and entry.** Prioritized product/evidence/trust/pilot links; connected footer actions to the real workspace; retained additional routes in a labeled footer disclosure. Evidence deep links open the intended section, tab changes synchronize their query parameter, unsupported/unauthorized section keys fall back to Setup, and accepted invitation tokens are not reintroduced into tab URLs.
8. **Reduced blocking presentation.** Removed the hero animation that hid the headline/CTA on arrival. Kept the established visual identity and landscape. Replaced the one-megabyte footer background with a lighter product footer and removed unused homepage modules from its import graph.
9. **Improved metadata honestly.** Distinct route titles and relevant descriptions, confirmed project name, and client-side noindex hints for workspace/research routes. Removed unconfirmed `carbonzerobd.com` OpenGraph/Twitter URLs. Social/canonical production URLs remain unconfigured until verified; this is not a complete crawl/indexing implementation.

No dependency, trained model, hosted service, external lead submission, payment, certification, or customer result was added by this increment.

## What “best” should mean before using that claim

Agree a target buyer and comparison task, then collect evidence on consented held-out documents and real users. At minimum: correct supported quantity/unit/period extraction, appropriate abstention, source completeness, factor applicability, review workload, user task success, reproducible export, permission isolation, recovery, and production operations. Compare like-for-like Scope 1/2 tasks and disclose excluded functionality. Establish customer preference and outcome evidence instead of assigning invented scores to competitors.
