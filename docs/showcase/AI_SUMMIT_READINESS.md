# CarbonOS: AI summit readiness audit

Date: 3 November 2026. Working branch: `Asif`.

## Event context and recommendation

The [official event site](https://aisummit.dhakachamber.com/) lists Bangladesh AI Business Summit 2026 for **5 November 2026 at Bangladesh-China Friendship Conference Center, Dhaka**. Its emphasis is practical business use, including SMEs, manufacturing, and agriculture. The [exhibitor page](https://aisummit.dhakachamber.com/exhibit/) describes day-long exhibition access in Milky Way. The [program](https://aisummit.dhakachamber.com/agenda/) does not establish this project's assigned booth, setup time, presentation slot, or allotted duration.

Recommendation: lead with **evidence-led ESG reporting for a Bangladesh factory**, then offer crop diagnosis and monitoring as secondary prototypes when their services are available. Use a three-minute walkthrough as an internal rehearsal target, not an organizer-issued time limit. The product story should be “source document → reviewed activity → traceable inventory → reproducible report.” Explain exactly where AI is used and where deterministic rules and human decisions take over.

The [exhibitor terms](https://aisummit.dhakachamber.com/refund-policy/) require approved products, professional booth conduct, safe electrical equipment, respect for intellectual property, and compliance with organizer instructions. This audit does not confirm exhibitor registration or allocation and does not accept those terms on the user's behalf.

## Current verdict

**A working prototype with a testable ESG demonstration, not a production-certified startup platform.**

The first implementation batch repairs the seeded inventory, completes its independent lock/export path, improves demo session reliability, adds an in-product walkthrough and sample bill, validates optional model responses, and corrects several high-visibility claims. Two synthetic live Gemini checks and a browser upload succeeded. Readiness still depends on repeatable rehearsal, provider availability, deployment choice, and the remaining gaps below. No revenue, customer, accuracy, regulatory, or assurance claims are established by this audit.

## Demonstration boundaries

- All showcased factory values and factors are synthetic fixtures. The expected 12.06 t Scope 1 and 80 t location-based Scope 2 explain the software path; they are not measured customer results or official factors.
- Market-based Scope 2 remains “Not reported” without approved market-based evidence. Scope 3 is not included.
- Record review, calculation, lock, and export use real server-side controls. Switching labeled demo identities simulates separate people; it is not evidence that an external auditor signed a real inventory.
- The default document example uses rule extraction and shows its source snippet. A configured LLM is optional and must be labeled and tested separately.
- The rest of the public marketing site still contains claims requiring review (including framework alignment, roadmap readiness, telemetry, and other specialist interfaces). Corrections to the main entry do not certify every page.

## Suggested two-day sequence

1. Finish and rehearse the verified ESG walkthrough, including error recovery and reset. Freeze new accounting features.
2. Decide whether a live AI call can be demonstrated honestly and reliably. If it cannot, describe the rules-based prototype and AI evaluation plan clearly; do not substitute fake model output.
3. Validate the display/laptop, internet, power, startup commands, and presentation backup. Confirm booth/setup instructions directly with the organizer.
4. Replace remaining unsupported public claims and confirm logo rights, team, pilot contact, public URL, and any QR materials.
5. Run the final smoke suite, capture genuine evidence, and freeze the presentation build. Expand production capabilities after the event with a measured pilot.
