## 1. Page Role & Intent

- **Primary Persona:** Institutional Auditor, Verification & Validation Body (VVB) Assessor, Sovereign Carbon Registry Official.
- **Cognitive Objective:** Immediate, unambiguous visual verification of carbon credit additionality, baseline counterfactuals, multi-pool distributions, and conformal uncertainty without calculation ambiguity.
- **Core Anti-Patterns to Eliminate:**
  - Equal-weight card grid causing audit decision fatigue.
  - Non-aligned decimal values in financial/crediting calculations.
  - Status conveyed solely by color without accessible icons or text.
  - Generic greenwashing aesthetics without granular empirical ground-truth proof.

---

## 2. Layout & Density Overrides (Density: 9/10)

- **Grid System:** 12-column responsive grid collapsing to single-column on mobile (< 768px).
- **Spacing Scale (Compact Dashboard):**
  - Section spacing: `space.6` (24px)
  - Card internal padding: `space.5` (12px to 20px)
  - Data row gaps: `space.3` (8px to 10px)
- **Hierarchy Allocation:**
  - **Hero Priority (Primary Card):** Card 4 (Conformal Uncertainty & Conservativeness) — accented with elevated emerald border (`border-emerald/40`), subtle glow, and visible RME threshold compliance meter (5% ≤ 15% threshold).
  - **Analytical Priority:** Card 2 (Multi-Pool Allometry) with visual pool distribution bar and Card 3 (Dynamic Baseline Additionality) with explicit math flow (`+`, `-`, `=`).
  - **Metadata Priority:** Card 1 (Geographic Boundary & Authority) and Card 5 (Earth Observation Sensors).
  - **Trust & Finality:** Card 6 (SHA-256 Ledger Provenance) and Card 7 (Sovereign VVB Attestation Seal).

---

## 3. Typography & Numerics

- **Headings:** Sans-serif bold (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`), tracking tight, sentence case with uppercase section tags.
- **Auditor Figures:** Strict `font-mono tabular-nums` right-aligned for all numeric units (`tC/ha`, `tCO₂e`, percentages, ha).
- **Hash Identifiers:** `font-mono text-xs select-all break-all` with copy-to-clipboard affordance.

## 5. Accessibility & Interaction (Motion: 3/10)

- **Keyboard Focus:** Every interactive control (Audit Run dropdown, PDF/Markdown/JSON download buttons, Verify Chain trigger, Hash copy button) has `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-emerald focus-visible:ring-offset-2`.
- **Screen Readers:** Status tags declare validation state (`aria-label="Chain integrity verified"`).
- **Motion:** Instant transitions (150ms) for hovers and tooltips; reduced motion respected (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`).
